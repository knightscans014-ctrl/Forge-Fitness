What you get

⚔️

Stats that mean something

Strength, endurance, agility and vitality all level independently from the work you actually put in.

📈

Ranks, not streaks

Progress from E-rank upward. Your power score is computed from real training volume, not from logging in.

Live leaderboard

Season rankings verified server-side. Scores are validated against your save — inflated numbers get rejected.

🐉

Guild raids

Team up and chip down a weekly boss. Damage comes from the sessions your guild completes.

☁️

Cloud save

Sign in and your progress follows you. Newest save wins, so a reinstall doesn't cost you months.

🔒

Server-verified

Premium status and rankings are decided by the server, not the app. Editing local files gets you nothing.

  1. Download the APK using the button below.
