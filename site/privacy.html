Privacy Policy

FORGE (com.forge.fitness) · Last updated 18 August 2026

This policy explains exactly what the FORGE mobile application collects, why it is collected, where it is stored, and how you can have it deleted. It is written to describe what the app actually does — not a generic template.

The short version. FORGE stores your email, a display name, your training progress, and — only if you buy premium — your UPI transaction reference. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. You can request deletion of everything at any time.

1. Who is responsible

FORGE is operated by an individual developer based in India. For any privacy question or a deletion request, contact knightscans014@gmail.com.

2. What we collect, and why

2.1 Account information

You can use FORGE's core training features without an account. Creating an account is required only for cloud save, the leaderboard, guilds, and premium.

DataWhy
Email addressIdentifies your account and lets you sign in and recover it.
Authentication identifier from Google (if you use Google Sign-In)Lets you sign in without a password. We do not receive your Google password.
Display name and chosen classShown on the leaderboard and to guild members. A display name is optional and does not have to be your real name.
Avatar image URL, if you set oneShown on your profile.

2.2 Training and game progress

DataWhy
Experience points, level, gold and power scoreThis is your saved game. It syncs so you keep progress across reinstalls and devices.
Season leaderboard scoreRanks you against other players. Validated server-side against your save.
Guild membership and raid contributionRuns the co-operative weekly boss feature.

Workout logs are used to compute your progress. FORGE does not request or collect location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, health-platform records, or your device's advertising identifier.

2.3 Payment information

Premium tiers are paid by UPI. Payment happens entirely inside your own UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or similar). FORGE never sees, requests, or stores card numbers, bank credentials, UPI PINs, or account numbers.

After paying, you enter a few details so the payment can be matched and verified:

DataWhy
UPI transaction reference (UTR)The only way to match your payment to your account, since verification is manual.
Name and email you supply with the paymentUsed to confirm the payment and contact you if it cannot be matched.
Tier purchased and amountDetermines what is unlocked, and forms the record of the transaction.
Status and review timestampShows whether your purchase is pending, approved or rejected.

Payment records are retained after approval because they are proof of purchase and may be needed for tax or dispute purposes.

2.4 Creator programme (optional)

If you apply for free premium as a creator, FORGE stores the email address and video link you submit, plus the review status. This is used only to assess your application.

2.5 App integrity

At launch FORGE performs a security check to confirm it is a genuine, unmodified installation. This uses the Google Play Integrity API and sends a short-lived attestation token to our verification service. The token confirms the app package and installation are legitimate. It is verified, used to allow or deny the session, and not retained as a profile of you or your device. This check protects the fairness of the leaderboard and prevents tampered builds from granting themselves premium.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or behavioural profiling services.
  • No sale, rental, or trade of personal data. Ever.
  • No sharing with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Where your data is stored

Account data and game data are stored with Supabase, which runs on managed cloud infrastructure and acts as our data processor. Access is enforced at the database level with row-level security: your records are readable only by your own authenticated account, and payment records are additionally visible only to the developer for the purpose of approving purchases.

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth, and by Google if you choose Google Sign-In. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.

A copy of your game save is also held locally on your own device so the app works offline. Uninstalling the app removes that local copy.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and save data — kept while your account exists, and deleted when you ask us to delete the account.
  • Leaderboard entries — kept for the duration of the season.
  • Payment records — retained after account deletion where required as a financial record, reduced to the transaction reference, amount and date.
  • Creator applications — deleted once reviewed, unless approved.

6. Your rights

You may at any time request that we:

  • tell you what data we hold about you;
  • provide a copy of it;
  • correct anything inaccurate;
  • delete your account and associated data.

Email knightscans014@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will action it within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and includes your game progress — it cannot be undone.

7. Children

FORGE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it. FORGE involves physical exercise; if you are under 18, use it with the agreement of a parent or guardian.

8. Not medical advice

FORGE is a fitness game, not a medical device or a source of medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any condition. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new exercise programme.

9. Distribution outside app stores

FORGE is distributed as a downloadable APK. Only install it from our official download page or our official GitHub releases page. Builds obtained elsewhere may have been modified, and this policy cannot describe what a modified build does.

10. Security

Access to your data is enforced by database-level row-level security rather than by the app alone, premium entitlements are granted only by the server, and leaderboard scores are validated against your stored save before being accepted. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your personal data we will notify affected users by email without undue delay.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated and, where the change is significant, you will be notified in the app. Continuing to use FORGE after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: knightscans014@gmail.com.
